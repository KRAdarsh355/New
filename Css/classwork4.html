Important Notice

Dear Students, We are delighted to announce that our Annual Sports Day will be held on 27th July at Sreekaryam Public School. This special event is an opportunity for all students to showcase their talents, teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship. All participants are requested to report to the venue by 11:00 am in the prescribed sports uniform. Students should bring their water bottles, wear appropriate sports shoes, and ensure they are well prepared for their respective events. Kindly follow the instructions of the teachers, officials, and volunteers throughout the program to ensure a safe and successful event. Students who are not participating in any event are encouraged to cheer for their classmates and help create a positive and energetic atmosphere. Let us all uphold the true spirit of sports by competing fairly, respecting one another, and giving our best effort. We look forward to your enthusiastic participation and cooperation in making this Sports Day a memorable success. Sports Day is not just about winning trophies or breaking records; it is a celebration of determination, teamwork, discipline, and the joy of healthy competition. Every event, whether on the track or the field, provides an opportunity for students to challenge themselves, discover their strengths, and build confidence. We encourage all participants to compete with honesty, respect the decisions of the officials, and appreciate the efforts of their fellow competitors. Students are advised to take good care of their health by staying hydrated, having a nutritious meal before the events, and taking adequate rest the previous night. Those participating in multiple events should report to the respective venues on time and be attentive to announcements regarding their events. Any student requiring assistance should immediately contact the teachers or volunteers on duty. All spectators are requested to encourage every participant with enthusiasm and positivity. Cheer for your classmates, appreciate good performances, and help create a lively and supportive atmosphere throughout the day. Let us remember that true sportsmanship lies in showing respect to opponents, celebrating victories with humility, and accepting defeats with grace. A spirit of unity, cooperation, and mutual encouragement will make this event enjoyable and memorable for everyone. We also request all students to maintain cleanliness and discipline throughout the program. Please dispose of waste only in the designated bins and avoid damaging the sports equipment or facilities. Follow the instructions of teachers, officials, and volunteers at all times to ensure the smooth conduct of the events. Your cooperation and responsible behavior will contribute greatly to the success of the program and create a safe and welcoming environment for all. May this Sports Day inspire every student to develop a lifelong appreciation for physical fitness, teamwork, perseverance, and fair play. Let us come together with enthusiasm, energy, and a positive attitude to celebrate the spirit of sports and create wonderful memories that we will cherish for years to come. We request all students to maintain discipline and punctuality throughout the event. Please keep the playground and surrounding areas clean by using the designated waste bins. Parents and guests are requested to cooperate with the organizers and encourage all participants equally. Let us remember that the true purpose of Sports Day is not only to win medals but also to develop confidence, perseverance, teamwork, and respect for one another. Every participant is a winner for taking part and giving their best effort. Thank you, and best wishes to all participants! favorite
